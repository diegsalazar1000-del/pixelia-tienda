# Prompts para Gemini (Veo) · Plan Financiero 2027 — v2 (Diego 11-nov: más impacto, cámara en movimiento, voz neutra con emoción, más sonido)

Reglas: 1 escena de ~8 s, movimientos de cámara cinematográficos, efectos de sonido marcados, voz en ESPAÑOL NEUTRO (sin acento regional), emoción fuerte, pantalla del teléfono nunca visible, final quieto 1 s para cortar. Sirve todo el año.

## A · El golpe de realidad (impacto)
Vertical 9:16 cinematic video, dramatic lighting with a cool blue tone that turns warm golden at the end. Extreme close-up of a crumpled payment receipt landing on a wooden table with a heavy, exaggerated thud sound. The camera quickly pulls back and rises (crane-up) to reveal a woman in her early 30s, sitting with her head in her hands, surrounded by scattered bills and receipts. Sound: a clock ticking loudly, a low tense bass drone. She lifts her head, looks straight into the lens with frustration and says in clear neutral Spanish (no regional accent, international broadcast style), with real emotion: "¿Otra vez sin plata… y apenas es quincena?" Then the camera pushes in fast toward her face (snap zoom) with a whoosh sound, the light shifts to warm gold, and she holds a determined expression, perfectly still, for one second. No text on screen, no music.

## B · Antes y después en un giro (efecto)
Vertical 9:16 cinematic video. Start on a man in his late 20s in a dim, messy room, staring at his phone (screen facing away from camera) with a worried face; sound of a phone notification buzz and a sad, low "whomp". The camera makes a fast 180-degree orbit around him with a strong whoosh; during the orbit the room becomes bright, tidy and sunny, and he is now relaxed, smiling, holding the same phone. Sound: a bright chime and a soft "cha-ching". He looks into the lens and says in clear neutral Spanish (no regional accent), excited and relieved: "Ahora sé exactamente en qué se me va la plata." The camera stops and he holds the smile, still, for one second. No text on screen, no music.

## C · El secreto entre amigas (curiosidad con emoción)
Vertical 9:16 cinematic video, warm afternoon light, shallow depth of field. Handheld camera slowly circling two women in their early 30s on a sofa. The first one gasps, eyes wide, grabs her friend's arm and asks with intense curiosity in clear neutral Spanish (no regional accent): "¡Espera! ¿Cómo hiciste para llegar a fin de mes sin deudas?" Sound: a dramatic record-scratch stop, then silence. The camera does a quick push-in to the second woman, who smiles proudly, turns her phone toward her friend (screen not visible to camera) and says: "Me hice un plan." Sound: a soft magical shimmer. Both look at the phone amazed and freeze, still, for one second. No text on screen, no music.
