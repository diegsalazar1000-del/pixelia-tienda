# Muñeca de papel Pixelia — prompts para Gemini (imágenes)

Reglas: TODO en el MISMO chat de Gemini (así guarda el estilo), formato vertical 2:3, fondo blanco puro, sin texto.
Estilo original de Pixelia (NO copiar personajes conocidos).

## 1. Muñeca base
Original anime chibi paper doll, Pixelia style: big head (about 1/3 of body height), large shiny eyes with two white highlights, small smile, soft pink blush, clean bold dark-purple outline, flat soft colors with gentle cel shading. Full body, front view, standing straight, feet slightly apart, arms slightly away from the body (not touching the torso), hands open. She is BALD (no hair at all, smooth round head) and wears only a simple white tank top and white shorts, barefoot. Centered, filling 85% of the image height, pure white background, no shadow on the floor, no text, no logo. Not based on any existing character.

## 2. Vestido (en el mismo chat, después de la muñeca)
Using the exact same doll from the previous image (same size, same position, same pose), dress her in a sunny yellow A-line dress with small white daisies and puffy short sleeves, knee length. Keep everything else identical.
→ Luego: Now show ONLY the dress, exactly the same size and position, with the doll removed, on pure white background. No body, no shadow.

## 3. Conjunto casual
Same doll, same size, same pose: pink t-shirt with a white heart and blue jeans, white sneakers. Keep everything else identical.
→ Luego: Now show ONLY the clothes (t-shirt, jeans and sneakers), same size and position, doll removed, pure white background.

## 4. Vestido de fiesta
Same doll, same size, same pose: turquoise party dress with a tulle skirt and small sparkles, thin straps, silver flats. Keep everything else identical.
→ Luego: Now show ONLY the party dress and shoes, same size and position, doll removed, pure white background.

## 5. Peinados (cada uno aparte)
Same doll, same size, same pose: give her long curly brown hair with a pink headband. Keep everything else identical.
→ Luego: Now show ONLY the hair (with the headband), same size and position, doll removed, pure white background. The hair must have an empty space where the face goes.

Repetir 5 con: "two black braids with yellow hair ties" y "high ponytail, copper red hair, side bangs".
